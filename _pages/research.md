<div class="page-content"><h1 class="page-title">Research</h1><p class="page-subtitle">Science of science · Computational social science · Network science</p>
<p>My research examines how social networks, status hierarchies, and cumulative advantage shape scientific careers, innovation, and recognition. I am especially interested in status mobility, the timing of scientific recognition, and how artificial intelligence may change scientific collaboration and social capital.</p>
<h2>Scientific recognition and status mobility</h2><p>I study how status hierarchies, scientific prizes, and cumulative advantage influence the timing and distribution of recognition across researchers’ careers.</p>
<h2>Scientific careers and mobility</h2><p>My work examines career trajectories, institutional prestige, and mobility. Current projects also consider researchers’ transitions from academia to industry and the longer-run effects of educational opportunities.</p>
<h2>Collaboration, social capital, and artificial intelligence</h2><p>I am interested in how collaboration networks and social capital support research, and whether the use of artificial intelligence changes scientists’ reliance on collaborators.</p>
<h2>Methods</h2><p>Computational social science, statistical analysis, network methods, and large-scale publication and citation data.</p>
